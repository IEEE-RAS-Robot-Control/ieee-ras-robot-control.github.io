IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation (ICRA) 2026 is around the corner, and the TC on Robot Control is excited to support a fantastic set of workshops and tutorials this year!

## Supported Workshops & Tutorials
* [Robust Manipulation under Uncertainty](https://lnkd.in/em5hTn5F)
* [Geometry in Robotics: Debates](https://lnkd.in/eqcBwcgM)
* [Human-Inspired Principles for Robotic Dexterous Manipulation: What Are We Missing? (June 5)](https://lnkd.in/eQFWbMRy)
* [Path Towards Generalizable Contact-Rich Robotics: Control and Representation (CR2)](https://cr2-icra.github.io)
* [3rd Tutorial on Geometric Methods in Robot Learning, Optimization and Control](https://lnkd.in/eEkgrSiu)
* [RoboTac: Embodied Tactile Intelligence in Predictive Perception, Learning & Control in Grasping & Manipulation](https://www.robotac.eu/robotac-2026)

## TC Social Event — Monday, June 1st, ~8:15pm
We are also co-hosting a social event together with the TC on Optimization for Robotics and the TC on Algorithms for Planning and Control of Robot Motion. 

A relaxed evening for discussions, food, drinks, and meeting fellow control enthusiasts from across the community! The venue is just a few steps from the conference center, with space for ~200 people.

**TC members who register in advance will receive free drink tickets.**
