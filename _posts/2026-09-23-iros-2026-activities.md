IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems (IROS) 2026 is around the corner, and the TC on Robot Control is excited to support a fantastic set of workshops and tutorials this year!

![IROS 2026 logo](/assets/images/conf_logos/iros_2026.svg){: .align-center style="width: 300px;"}

## Workshops & Tutorials Around IROS 2026

### Sunday, Sep. 27
* [**Hybrid Architectures for Embodied Autonomy: Bridging Learning, Planning, and Control**](https://sites.google.com/bu.edu/hybridarchforautonomy){:target="_blank"}  
  Half Day · 1:30 PM–5:30 PM · [Rooms 303 & 304](https://2026.ieee-iros.org/program/venue-map/?room=303%2F304#level-3){:target="_blank"}

### Thursday, Oct. 1
* [**Sim2Real and Classical Control: From Rigorous Theory to Data-Driven Robotics**](https://sim2realgap.github.io/sim2real-and-control-workshop-iros2026/){:target="_blank"}  
  Full Day · 8:30 AM–5:30 PM · [Rooms 303 & 304](https://2026.ieee-iros.org/program/venue-map/?room=303%2F304#level-3){:target="_blank"}
* [**Contact & Learning: Is the structure of contact still useful for solving manipulation and locomotion challenges?**](https://sites.google.com/view/contact-and-learning/home){:target="_blank"}  
  Full Day · 8:30 AM–5:30 PM · [Room 406](https://2026.ieee-iros.org/program/venue-map/?room=406#level-4){:target="_blank"}
* [**AI Meets Control for Resilient and Reliable Robot Planning**](https://sites.google.com/view/aim-ctrl/home-page){:target="_blank"}  
  Half Day · 8:30 AM–12:30 PM · [Rooms 408 & 409](https://2026.ieee-iros.org/program/venue-map/?room=408%2F409#level-4){:target="_blank"}
* [**Bridging Learning and Physics for Robust Whole-Body Robot Control**](https://sites.google.com/view/iros2026ws-wbc/home){:target="_blank"}

* [**Learning-to-Optimize for Control in Robotics**](https://ctrl-l2o-iros2026.github.io/Ctrl-L2O-IROS2026/){:target="_blank"}

## Keynotes

### Tuesday, Sep. 29 — Afternoon Keynotes
* [**How you can be part of a revolution in composite manufacturing**](https://2026.ieee-iros.org/program/keynotes/#kn-tue-1-1){:target="_blank"}  
  Tim Bretl (University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign), 5:00 PM
* [**Big bets in manipulation systems: where is the frontier today?**](https://2026.ieee-iros.org/program/keynotes/#kn-tue-1-2){:target="_blank"}  
  Lael Odhner (RAI Institute), 5:30 PM

### Wednesday, Sep. 30 — Afternoon Keynotes
* [**Lorentz Levitation Technology for Fine Motion Robotics, Haptic Interaction, and Vibration Isolation**](https://2026.ieee-iros.org/program/keynotes/#kn-wed-1-0){:target="_blank"}  
  Ralph Hollis (Carnegie Mellon University), 4:30 PM

And throughout the technical program, you will find sessions on contact-rich manipulation, whole-body control, safety in learned control, legged loco-manipulation, control barrier functions, dynamics-grounded control, adaptive and differentiable control, compliant robots, optimal control, and much more.

## TC Social Event — Tuesday, Sep. 29, 9:00 PM
On Tuesday evening, we are joining forces again with the [TC on Optimization for Robotics](https://www.tcoptrob.org/){:target="_blank"} and the [TC on Algorithms for Planning and Control of Robot Motion](https://www.ieee-ras.org/algorithms-for-planning-and-control-of-robot-motion/){:target="_blank"}.

A relaxed evening to meet fellow control, optimization, and planning enthusiasts from across the community!
